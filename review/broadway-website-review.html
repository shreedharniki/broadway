Broadway Communication Pvt. Ltd.

Website review

The current live demo and the new build, assessed for UI/UX, page flow and marketing impact, with a three-week plan to launch.

UI/UX and marketing assessmentOctober 2026

Executive summary

The new build is a much stronger brand. It still needs the sales basics.

The new build makes Broadway look like an established infrastructure partner; the live demo looks like a bought template. But the new build dropped pricing, click-to-call, WhatsApp and the PIN availability check, which home buyers rely on. Neither site currently delivers enquiries anywhere.

Live demo today6.0Template look, forms not connected
New build7.5Premium brand, missing pricing and calls

Two sites, one method

Live demo

codemythought.in/demo/broadway. PHP site on a bought jQuery theme. Homepage plus 15 inner pages, all loading correctly.

New build

Full-screen home page built from scratch in React, Vite, Tailwind and GSAP. Run locally for this review.

Method

Walkthroughs at desktop (1366px) and mobile (375px) widths; page code, forms, links, metadata and load timing; plus three structured audits of the new build (UX heuristics, an automated anti-pattern scan, a six-pillar visual review).

Page flow

  • The hero carousel rotates three different pitches (CCTV, leased lines, fiber), so each visitor sees a different message.
  • Plans start about 2,800px down the page, below eight large service cards. Price is a home buyer's first question.
  • Testimonials come after the forms and are anonymous ("Home User, Bangalore Resident").
  • On mobile the page is about 13,600px long, roughly 17 screens, with no sticky call bar.
  • About ten different button labels compete for attention.

Part 2 · New build

What was done

City hero

A full-screen connected Bengaluru skyline. Services rise from the rooftops as you scroll, then the view dives into the network hub.

One message

"Connecting lives. Powering possibilities." with two clear actions: Explore services and Discuss your requirements.

Six service families

Internet, networks and Wi-Fi, security and automation, voice and intercom, IT and cloud, entertainment.

Who we serve

Homes, apartments and communities, businesses, and institutions each get tailored copy and a key outcome.

Delivery process

Survey, design, install, support: shows how the work actually happens.

Pre-filled enquiry

Every Explore or Enquire button scrolls to the form with the service and audience already chosen.

It also adds service detail panels, a separate mobile hero crop, keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, a reduced-motion mode, and Open Graph tags for link previews.

Buyers judge the vendor by the website

Credibility with enterprise

IT parks, hospitals and campuses read a template site as a small operator.

A message people remember

One story replaces three rotating slides that competed for attention.

Show the real advantage

"One local partner for everything" is now shown as one network across the city, not a product list.

Room to grow

A modern codebase makes new pages, offers and locality pages quick to add; the old theme loaded 26 jQuery plugins.

Talking points for the client

  • You now look like the established infrastructure partner you are, not a reseller.
  • One message, one story: Bengaluru connected by one accountable team.
  • Every visitor finds their own path: home, apartment, business or campus.
  • Every button leads to a pre-filled enquiry, so there is less friction before the conversation starts.
  • It is built on a modern stack, so the next pages and offers are quick to add.

Part 4 · Structured audits

Three audits agree: a strong base with clear gaps

The new build was checked with three independent methods. They grade on different scales but point at the same problems.

UX heuristics (Nielsen's 10)20/40

Acceptable. Weakest: help and efficiency, at 1 of 4 each, because there is no phone, FAQ or quick way to get in touch.

Technical audit9/20

Poor. Responsive layout is good (3 of 4); colour tokens and visual clichés score 1 of 4.

Six visual pillars17/24

Good. Typography is weakest (2 of 4); copy, visuals, colour, spacing and experience score 3 of 4.

Bring back what home buyers use

Plans and prices

With GST, installation charge, router, data limits and the OTT apps included.

PIN check near the top

Ask for the PIN first and confirm coverage, then ask for name and phone.

Call and WhatsApp

In the header and as a sticky bar on mobile: Call, WhatsApp, Check availability.

FAQ near the form

Installation time, router setup, leased line versus home plan.

Real address and areas

The Yelahanka office and a list or map of localities served.

Two button labels

"Check availability" for homes and "Get a quote" for business, used everywhere.

Trust, tracking and local search

  • Connect the form to email, a CRM or a Google Sheet, with a thank-you page and GA4 and Meta conversion events.
  • Replace anonymous testimonials with named customers, apartment or company names, and Google reviews.
  • Add real numbers (customers, buildings connected, uptime) and client and RWA logos.
  • Add LocalBusiness schema, a Google Business Profile link and a map.
  • Add a path for existing customers: pay bill, raise a ticket, speed test.
  • Build locality landing pages such as Yelahanka, Hebbal and Whitefield.
  • Use real photography of the team, vans and installations.
  • Link the social icons to real profiles, or remove them.

Part 6 · Plan

Three weeks to a site that sells

Week 1 · must fix

  • Connect forms; thank-you page and tracking
  • Real contact details, verified claims
  • Remove the 7.9 MB preload
  • Fix the Support link

Week 2 · conversion

  • Plans with the fine print
  • PIN availability check
  • Sticky Call and WhatsApp bar
  • Two button labels
  • Shorter scroll, no input lock
  • Fix contrast; quieter glow and glass

Week 3 · trust and SEO

  • Named testimonials, Google reviews
  • Logos and real numbers
  • Type scale and colour tokens
  • Business schema and map
  • Locality pages; pay bill and support entry

We rebuilt the brand to match Broadway's standing. Next, we add the home-buyer layer and connect the leads, so the site sells as well as it looks.
